01 — kwel

kwel_playground is a Hong Kong artist building dark, surreal worlds with AI — creepy-cute dolls, neon-soaked nightmares, and gothic dreamscapes where the adorable and the uncanny collide. Every piece is a small, haunted playground. kwel_playground 是一位香港藝術家，用 AI 建構暗黑而超現實的世界——詭異可愛的娃娃、霓虹浸染的夢魘、哥德式的夢境，讓可愛與詭異在此碰撞。每一件作品，都是一個小小的、鬧鬼的遊樂場。
